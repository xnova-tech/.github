## About XNova

XNova explores technology through clear explanations, in-depth articles, hands-on learning, and reproducible projects.

XNova is an independent technology brand. Its materials cover the mechanisms, decisions, and trade-offs behind real technical work.

Articles explain ideas in depth; courses and open projects give readers practical examples they can run and inspect.
